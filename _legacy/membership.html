? FAQ

What do I get?

The full lesson library, a new ~10-minute lesson every week, the written weekly routine and BPM targets for each one, Tracks, monthly challenges, and your practice log. Every plan gets everything.

How much do I have to practice?

15 minutes a day is the minimum that works. One lesson is a full week. Don’t take more than two at once.

Is this a 30-day course?

No. It’s a weekly lesson library — you choose what to work on. If you want a path, take a Track: four lessons in order, about a month.

Is this official Travis Barker or blink-182 content?

No. Mike teaches in the style of that pocket from years of playing it live. No affiliation or endorsement, and we teach to a click or our own backing tracks — never over the records.

How is this different from YouTube?

YouTube gives you a video. This gives you a routine, a tempo target, a retest, and a leaderboard — plus the guy who filmed it answering you.

Do I need the book?

No. The membership stands alone. Founding annual includes the PDF.

What if I’m a beginner?

Fine. Every lesson has a slow tempo entry point, and the challenge leaderboard ranks improvement, not speed.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, from your account — Stripe’s portal, no email required.

Can I get feedback on my playing?

Post your take in the member community and in the monthly challenge. Mike watches those. Structured 1:1 review comes once we’re past 100 members.
