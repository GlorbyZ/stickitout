# Stick It Out rudiment tempos: old vs new (review)

## Final numbers after migration 0011 (every tempo rounded up to a 10)

Applied 2026-09-26. Every Diamond goal, start tempo and tier threshold is rounded UP to the next 10 (values already on a 10 stay). Tier thresholds are `ceil10(Diamond x share)`, so the percentages are the starting point and the displayed number is always on a 10. Rounding up nudges some rudiments closer to singles than the ratio alone (for example doubles 171 became 180).
