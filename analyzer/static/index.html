Your camera preview shows up here with a live skeleton over your shoulders, elbows, wrists and hands.

The skeleton is drawn only on screen. The recorded file is the plain camera video with sound. Use a tripod, side angle preferred, good light, and keep your full torso, both arms and the pad in view.

Recording for training (clips for Zaylyn and Mike to label)
  • Side angle, phone level with the pad or drum, 2 to 3 m away, on a stand.
  • Both arms and both sticks in frame the whole time, in good light.
  • 60 fps preferred. Start with one clap (hands in view), keep a click running, play 30 to 45 seconds.
  • After analyzing, press Add to training set on the results, then label it on the Label page. The shot list of about 20 takes is on the Label page and in docs/training-shot-list.md.

Tip: 60 fps is best. 30 fps works too, but fast strokes can fall between frames, so some scores will be estimates. Good light helps the camera keep 60 fps.
