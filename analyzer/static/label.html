Recording for training
  • Side angle, phone level with the pad or drum, 2 to 3 m away.
  • Both arms and both sticks in frame the whole time, plus the pad or snare.
  • Good, even light. No bright window behind the player.
  • Phone on a stand or tripod, not hand held.
  • 60 fps preferred (1080p at 60 fps). 30 fps works but is less accurate.
  • One clap at the start with hands in view, then count in.
  • Click running and audible on the recording. Note its BPM.
  • 30 to 45 seconds per take.

Shot list (about 20 takes): pad and snare; slow (70), medium (100) and fast; singles, doubles and paradiddles; one accent take and one ghost note take; and some deliberately sloppy takes: flipped sticking, dragging behind the click, one hand dropping out, uneven doubles, and a mixed one. The full list is in docs/training-shot-list.md.

Label training clips

Pick a clip on the left, or add one. Each clip opens with the analyzer's own strokes already marked, so you only fix what it got wrong: missing or extra strokes, the wrong hand, accents and ghost notes, and sticking mistakes.

Labels are saved next to the video in the dataset folder and used by scripts/evaluate.py to measure the analyzer.

How to label and keyboard shortcuts
  1. Watch at 0.25x or 0.5x. Each marker is a stroke: L on the top lane, R on the bottom, unknown hand in the middle.
  2. Tap F (left) or J (right) on every stroke. Tapping on an existing marker changes its hand, so you can tap along to fix hands. A tap where nothing is marked adds a stroke.
  3. Click a marker to select it, then fix it: A accent, G ghost, X sticking mistake, H swap hand, Del delete. Drag it to move it; drag it to the other lane to change the hand.
  4. Mark the clap with C. Fill in the clip details, then Save and mark done.
