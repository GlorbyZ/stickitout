# Training shot list

About 20 takes that give the analyzer a balanced set of real clips to be measured and tuned
on. Zaylyn and Mike Staus can each record the list (two players are better than one), and
the takes can be spread over a few sessions. BPM values are quarter-note clicks with 16th
notes played on top (4 strokes per click), so slow is 70, medium is 100 and fast is as fast
as you can play cleanly (about 120 to 140).

## Set up for every take

- **Side angle.** Phone level with the pad or drum, 2 to 3 m away, looking at the player's side.
- **Both arms and both sticks in frame** for the whole take, plus the pad or snare.
- **Good, even light.** Face a window or lamp; avoid a bright window behind the player.
- **Phone on a stand or tripod,** never hand held.
- **60 fps preferred** (iPhone: Settings > Camera > Record Video > 1080p at 60 fps). 30 fps works but is less accurate.
- **One clear clap at the start,** hands in view, then count in. The clap lines up sound and picture.
- **Click running** and loud enough to be heard on the recording. Write down its BPM.
- **30 to 45 seconds** of playing per take.
- Name the file with the take number, for example `T08-pad-paradiddle-100.mov`, and fill in
  the clip details on the label page (rudiment, click BPM, surface, camera angle, and what went wrong on purpose).

## After recording

1. Open the label page (`/label` on the analyzer link) and upload each take with **Add a clip**,
   or analyze it on the main page and press **Add to training set** on the results.
2. Label it (see the README section "Labeling a clip"), fill in the details, and mark it **Done**.
3. Mike: give each take a form grade from 1 to 10 with a short comment.
